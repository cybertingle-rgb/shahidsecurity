## What to look for

- Unexpected admin users in **Users → All Users**
- New scheduled tasks or plugins you didn't install
- Search engines flagging the site as "hacked" in results
- Outbound spam emails from your hosting account

## First steps

1. Take the site offline or put it into maintenance mode.
2. Change every password: WordPress admin, hosting, database, FTP/SFTP.
3. Take a backup of the compromised state before cleaning anything (for forensics).
4. Update WordPress core, theme and all plugins once you're ready to clean up.
