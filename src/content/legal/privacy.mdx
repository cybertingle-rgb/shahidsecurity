## Who we are

Shahid Security ("we", "us") is a cybersecurity consultancy based in Islamabad, Pakistan. This policy explains what information we collect through shahidiqbal.com and what we do with it.

## What we collect

When you use our contact form or booking page, we collect:

- Your name and email address
- Your company and phone/WhatsApp number, if provided
- The service you're interested in and the message you send us
- Whether you've asked for an NDA before sharing details

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics by default.

## How we use it

We use the information you send us only to respond to your enquiry, schedule a consultation, and — if you engage us — to deliver the agreed work. We never sell your data.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and contact form data only as long as needed to respond to you and, if you become a client, for the duration of our engagement plus a reasonable retention period for records. [FILL IN: confirm your actual retention period.]

## Third parties

We use the following third-party services to operate this site and process enquiries:

- **Resend** — to deliver contact form emails
- **Cloudflare Turnstile** — to prevent spam submissions
- **Cal.com** — to power the consultation booking calendar on `/book/`

Each of these providers processes limited data (such as your email address or form submission) solely to provide their service to us.

You can ask us to tell you what data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, by emailing [info@shahidiqbal.com](mailto:info@shahidiqbal.com).
