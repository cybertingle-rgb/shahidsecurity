included:
  - Web application testing (OWASP Top 10)
  - API testing
  - Authentication and session review
  - Business-logic testing
  - Network / external infrastructure testing
  - Optional mobile app testing
deliverables:
  - Executive summary for management
  - Technical findings with severity (CVSS), proof and fix steps
  - Free re-test after remediation
  - Letter of attestation
process:
  - title: Free consultation
    text: 30 minutes to understand your setup and concerns.
  - title: Scoped proposal
    text: Fixed scope, fixed price, clear timeline. NDA signed before any access.
  - title: Testing & review
    text: We test safely, with written authorisation, inside agreed boundaries.
  - title: Report & fix
    text: Plain-language report, prioritised by risk, and a free re-test after you fix.
methodology:
  - 'Reconnaissance and scoping: confirm targets, environment and rules of engagement in writing before any testing begins.'
  - 'Automated discovery: a baseline scan for known vulnerabilities and misconfigurations, used to guide manual testing — not as the deliverable itself.'
  - 'Manual exploitation: hands-on testing against OWASP Top 10 categories, authentication, session handling and business logic that scanners miss.'
  - 'Safe validation: findings are proven with evidence (screenshots, requests, logs) without destructive techniques against production data.'
  - 'Reporting and re-test: findings are written up by severity, then re-tested free of charge once you have applied fixes.'
expectedOutcomes:
  - "A clear, CVSS-rated list of what's actually exploitable — not just what a scanner flagged."
  - 'A report you can hand to your own developers, a client, or an auditor without translation.'
  - 'Confirmation that your fixes actually closed the gap, via the included re-test.'
limitations:
  - 'A penetration test reflects your security posture at the time of testing — new code or configuration changes afterward are not covered until the next test.'
  - 'Testing is limited to the scope and systems agreed in writing; anything outside that scope is not tested.'
  - 'No test, automated or manual, can guarantee a system is 100% secure — the goal is to close as many real, exploitable gaps as possible within the agreed scope.'
faqs:
  - q: Will testing break my site?
    a: We agree test windows and avoid destructive techniques. Production testing is optional; staging is preferred.
  - q: How long does it take?
    a: Typically 5-10 working days for a standard web app, depending on scope.
  - q: Automated scan or manual?
    a: Manual testing supported by tools. Scanner-only reports miss logic flaws.
