Software-as-a-service businesses carry a specific kind of risk that generic "website security" advice doesn't cover: the product itself is the attack surface, every customer's data sits in the same systems as every other customer's, and a growing enterprise pipeline increasingly depends on being able to answer hard security questions credibly.
