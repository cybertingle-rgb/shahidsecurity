## Tier 5 — Earn backlinks, don't just submit to them (the highest-quality, hardest-won kind)

Directory listings are the easy, mechanical part. The links that actually move rankings the most come from other sites choosing to cite you, which only happens when there's something worth citing. Two concrete things already sitting on this site are genuinely link-worthy right now:

- **Pitch the `cPGuard/OPSSHIELD supply-chain attack` post** (`/blog/cpguard-opsshield-supply-chain-attack-credential-rotation/`) to hosting-industry outlets and cPanel-community forums — it's a real, well-sourced, currently-relevant incident writeup that those communities are actively discussing right now, and a genuinely useful one to link to.

## A note on what "security" means for this specific task

You asked me to keep the site secure while doing this. The relevant risk here isn't code — it's **account and reputation risk**: directory accounts tied to `info@shahidiqbal.com`, consistent NAP data that scammers could otherwise spoof, and a real temptation in the backlink-building industry to use shady "SEO link packages." The concrete rule that protects against all of it: every listing above is created and verified directly by you (or someone you trust with that one login), using an email you control — never handed to a third-party "SEO service" that asks for your site's FTP, CMS, or DNS access to "add some links for you." That request, specifically, is the single most common way a legitimate small business site ends up either penalized or compromised in the name of SEO.
