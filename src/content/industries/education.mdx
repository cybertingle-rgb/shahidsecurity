Educational institutions sit in an unusual position: they hold large volumes of sensitive personal data, run increasingly complex connected-device deployments, and are expected to defend against the same threats as a well-resourced enterprise — on a fraction of the budget.

Our [IoT and smart-campus threat-analysis case study](/case-studies/iot-smart-campus-threat-analysis/) covers exactly this kind of connected-device risk assessment in a real campus environment.
