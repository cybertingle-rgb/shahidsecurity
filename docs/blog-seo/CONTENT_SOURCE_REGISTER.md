## Honesty notes

- No CVE, statistic, vendor name, or publication date in any of the 10 articles was invented. Every figure traces to a source above.
- Where sources disagreed (insider-threat stats, AI-code-vulnerability percentages, the Magecart "103% surge" figure), the articles present the *range* and flag the inconsistency rather than picking one number and stating it as settled fact.
- No testimonial, client story, or case study appears as a real event. The one narrative-style article (`anatomy-of-a-website-compromise-case-study`) is explicitly and repeatedly labeled as an illustrative, hypothetical composite scenario, not a real client incident.
- No article claims Shahid Security discovered, privately tested, or has first-hand incident experience with any vulnerability or technique discussed — all are written as advisory/educational content citing third-party research.
- This register should be re-checked against primary sources (especially the NIST documents, OWASP's own cheat sheet, and the original Ponemon/DTEX/Verizon DBIR reports) before being treated as a permanent record, since several entries above explicitly note that only secondary coverage was available this session.
