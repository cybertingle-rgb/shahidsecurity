## 2. What information we collect

We only collect information you actively choose to give us. We don't use
tracking cookies, analytics scripts or third-party advertising pixels on
this Site, and we don't require an account to browse it.

**Contact form** (`/contact/`): name, email address, company (optional),
phone/WhatsApp number (optional), the service you're interested in, your
message, and whether you've requested an NDA.

**Booking form** (`/book/`): name, country, phone/WhatsApp number, and your
preferred consultation date and time. We deliberately don't collect an email
address here — requests are confirmed by phone or WhatsApp.

**If you become a client**: additional information necessary to deliver the
agreed work — for example, systems in scope for a penetration test, technical
contacts, or credentials explicitly provided under a signed authorisation
letter and NDA. This is governed by the specific engagement agreement, not
just this policy.

**Server logs**: like virtually any web server, ours may record basic
technical data (IP address, browser type, timestamp, requested page) for
security and troubleshooting. We don't cross-reference this with form
submissions to build visitor profiles.

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18, and this
Site is not directed at children.

## 3. Why we collect it and our legal basis

We process the information above to:

- Respond to your enquiry or booking request
- Schedule and conduct a consultation
- Deliver agreed security or development work, if you engage us
- Meet legal or contractual obligations (for example, keeping records
  required for invoicing or an engagement's audit trail)

Our legal basis is your consent (submitting the form), the steps necessary
to enter into or perform a contract with you, or our legitimate interest in
running and securing this Site — whichever applies to the specific
processing activity.

## 5. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share limited
data only with the service providers needed to operate this Site and respond
to you:

- **Hostinger** — our web host, which also delivers contact and booking form
  emails via SMTP on our behalf.
- **Cloudflare Turnstile** — a bot-verification service that may process
  your IP address and browser signals when you submit a form, solely to
  distinguish humans from automated spam.

Each provider only receives the minimum data needed to perform its function
for us, and is contractually or by nature of its service restricted from
using that data for its own purposes.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our legal
rights, or to investigate fraud or a security incident.

## 6. International data transfers

Our hosting and email-delivery infrastructure may process data outside
Pakistan depending on where our hosting provider's servers are located. Where
that happens, the provider is responsible for applying appropriate
safeguards under its own terms of service.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect the data you send us,
including transport encryption (HTTPS), server-side input validation on both
forms, spam/bot filtering, and rate-limiting. No online transmission or
storage system is ever 100% secure — as a security company, we're the first
to say so — but we do not store form submissions in a database on this Site
(there isn't one); the only record is the email your submission generates and,
if you become a client, records within our contract-management process.

If we ever become aware of a data breach affecting your personal
information, we will notify you and any relevant authority as required by
applicable law.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you're based, you may have the right to:

- Ask what personal data we hold about you
- Ask us to correct inaccurate data
- Ask us to delete your data (subject to any legal obligation to retain it)
- Object to or restrict certain processing
- Ask for a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these, email [info@shahidiqbal.com](mailto:info@shahidiqbal.com).
We'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

## 9. Cookies and tracking

This Site does not set tracking or advertising cookies. It may use
strictly-necessary session mechanisms (for example, within the Cloudflare
Turnstile widget) required for the contact and booking forms to function
securely — these do not track you across other websites.
